Which ESM-2 layer reflects RNA binding similarity of RBPs?

Dmitrii Zakharenko, 19.11.26. Test on RNAcompete data; protein embeddings are frozen ESM-2 650M.

Method

  1. Binding vector per protein: published RNAcompete 7-mer Z-scores (16,382 7-mers; mean over the experiments of the same protein sequence); 175 proteins.
  2. Binding-similarity matrix: Pearson correlation between the 175 vectors.
  3. Embedding-similarity matrices: mean-pooled residue embeddings of every ESM-2 layer (0-33), cosine similarity, raw and centred over proteins. Proteins longer than 1,022 residues would use windows of 1,022 with overlap 511 (none of the 175 is that long).
  4. Per layer: Spearman correlation between the upper triangles of the two matrices (15,225 protein pairs); Mantel permutation test (500 permutations).

  • All layers correlate positively with binding similarity (rho 0.07-0.16); Mantel p = 0.002 for every layer (smallest value possible with 500 permutations).
  • Maximum: layer 32 for raw cosine (0.132), layer 22 for centred cosine (0.158).
  • The last layer (33), used by the frozen heads, is lower: 0.087 raw, 0.100 centred.
  • Layer 0 (token embedding, i.e. amino-acid composition) reaches 0.112 / 0.119, similar to most deep layers.

Limits

  • The permutation test compares each layer with zero; differences between layers are not tested unless intervals are drawn above.
  • Protein pairs are not independent (each protein is in 174 pairs). Several proteins belong to the same families.
  • The whole protein sequence is pooled, not the RNA-binding domain only.
