H7. Cross-protein negatives in training not supported

Hypothesis
Training with the same RNA paired with another protein (composition-neutral negatives) forces the model to learn the interaction.
Test
V4 with this objective (script 65, early stopping on a held-out cross-protein probe of val proteins); frozen bilinear head with the same objective.

One-hot V4: training loss falls from 1.36 to 0.77 while the validation probe stays at 0.49-0.52 (best 0.522 at epoch 2, early stop at epoch 10): the model memorises training pairs. Frozen bilinear head (3 seeds): probe 0.559 +/- 0.010 with this objective vs 0.531 +/- 0.027 with ordinary labels, i.e. +0.03 but still 0.07 below nn1; its standard-test AUROC falls from 0.623 to 0.531 because the objective removes the RNA-composition shortcut.

Caveats. One seed, one set of hyper-parameters for the one-hot model.

H11. Number of training clusters partly supported

Hypothesis
Transfer to unseen clusters grows with the number of training clusters.
Test
Bilinear head with cross-protein negatives trained on 25/50/75/100% of the 160 clusters.

Probe AUROC by fraction of training clusters (seeds 42 / 0; 100%: seeds 42 / 0 / 1): 25% 0.535 / 0.521, 50% 0.554 / 0.541, 75% 0.549 / 0.537, 100% 0.570 / 0.553 / 0.554. Means 0.528, 0.548, 0.543, 0.559: +0.03 over a fourfold increase of clusters, with seed-to-seed differences of 0.01-0.02. The curve does not approach the nn1 level (0.627) and cannot be extrapolated reliably.

Caveats. Cluster sampling is dominated by the one large cluster (220 RRM proteins), so the fractions are not comparable in protein count; two seeds per fraction.
